# User guide

StepUp works in the browser (installable PWA) and as an Android app with the same screens.

## Settings
Choose the language (Hebrew or English) and the look (automatic, light or dark). The version number is shown at the bottom.

## Walk or run
On Home choose **Start a walk** or **Start a run**, wait for "GPS ready" and press **Start**. You see time, distance and pace. **Pause** stops the clock and the distance; **Finish** asks first and then shows your summary and the route. The lock button on the top corner locks the screen: press and hold the button to unlock. If the app is closed in the middle of a workout, Home offers **Continue**.

## Rhythm coach (metronome)
During a workout tap the dial button. It first listens quietly to your steps. When your steps are steady it begins to click at your own pace and shows a calm circle that pulses with every step and breathes with you ("Breathe in / out", by default in for 3 steps, out for 2). It tells you if you are a little fast or slow and follows you when you settle into a new pace. Use the arrows to nudge the rhythm. If the phone has no motion sensor, the rhythm you set in Settings is used. Choose the breathing pattern and volume in Settings.

## Dashboard
The home screen shows your **Start a walk / run** buttons and widgets: weekly effort, steps, distance, pace, consistency, weather and performance, personal bests and alerts. **Tap any widget** to open its details with a bigger chart and the workouts behind it. Use the filters (period, activity, weather, text) to narrow what you see. **Customise** lets you hide widgets and move them up or down; your layout is saved.

## Effort bar and alerts
Your weekly effort bar is built from your age and what you really did in the last weeks. It rises gently, never more than about 10 % a week. Running counts double, brisk walking once, an easy stroll less. If a workout is far beyond your plan (much longer or faster than usual, or a very heavy week) you get an alert that explains what happened, why it matters and what to do, and you can raise the bar gently with one tap. Turn these alerts off in Settings → Weekly effort. This is general guidance, not medical advice.

## Steps
Steps are estimated from your tracked walks and runs. Brisk, unbroken stretches count more than short or slow ones. The recommendation for today follows your own recent pattern.

## Reminders
Settings → Reminders: add reminders for a walk or a movement break at set times and days. Turn on **Smart nudges** to get a gentle prompt when there was no movement for a long time (only in your waking hours, a few times a day at most, never during a workout). Allow notifications to also see them outside the app. For now reminders arrive while StepUp is open.

## Quick actions, routes and the live map
At the top of the dashboard: **Measure now** starts a measurement immediately (distance and your path, shown on a small live map), **My routes** opens your routes, and the **suggested route** is the best route for now.
After a walk or run StepUp recognises a route you already saved, or offers to **save the path as a route**. Mark a route **one-time** (a trip or holiday) and it is ignored in suggestions. On a route's page you see your progress (pace chart, how much faster you are than in your first times, where you do best by time of day and weather), your attempts, and **Start this route**: the route is drawn and a ghost of your best attempt shows whether you are ahead or behind. You can also rename, delete a route (workouts stay), or link your earlier workouts to it. Route suggestions favour the routes where you perform best at this time of day and in this weather, and that suit your usual distance.
The map shows your path without a background map for now.

## Recommendations
The star in the top bar opens **Recommendations**. Choose walk or run and see the best times today or tomorrow: your usual times when the weather suits them, good times by weather alone, and times better to avoid (heat, rain, wind, dark), with the reason. It learns your routine from the last weeks, so if you usually walk at 7 and 18 it suggests those hours first. The same screen has recommendations from your effort bar, steps and streak, and the dashboard has a Recommendations widget that opens it.
Weather needs the internet. The first time, StepUp asks for your permission; only your approximate location (about 1 km) is sent to the free service Open-Meteo, and nothing else. Settings → Recommendations lets you turn it off ("Forget my location") or get a notification before your usual time when the weather is good.

## Search
The magnifier searches everything: settings, screens, widgets, workouts and alerts, in Hebrew or English. Tap a result to go there.

## Weather
Open a workout and add the weather. The weather widget and the filter then show how you perform in cold, mild, hot or rainy conditions.

## History
Every finished workout is saved on the phone. Open it to see the route, the pace and each kilometre, or delete it.

## Look
Settings → Look: eight colour looks, each with light and dark; "High contrast" is best in strong sunlight.
