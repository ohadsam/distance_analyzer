# THE TASK: ONE ORIGINAL CHARACTER, BORN FROM THE STEPUP LOGO, THAT ACCOMPANIES THE APP EVERYWHERE A PERSON IS NEEDED

The walking / running figures I have now are generic. I want a **signature character that is unmistakably StepUp: the logo, brought to life as a person.** It must look designed, polished, warm, clear at 24 px and beautiful at 400 px, and move with charm.

## 1. The logo it comes from (use its exact geometry as the character's DNA)
The StepUp mark (viewBox 64x64) is three shapes: **a teal rounded hill** (#006B60, left, tall and soft), **a green rounded hill** (#216B39, right, lower and wider), separated by **an S-shaped gap - the trail** - and **a warm amber sun disc** (#865000) above them. The SVG paths of the mark are:

Build the character from these shapes, recognisably: **the sun disc is the HEAD**, **the teal hill is the TORSO and back** (the rounded shoulder line of the hill is the shoulders), **the green hill becomes the LEGS and the lower body** (its two soft lobes read as the two legs when moving), and **the S-shaped trail gap becomes the flowing ribbon / scarf / the path of motion** that follows the character (it is the "trail" in Little Trail). Arms are short rounded limbs in the teal and green (rounded capsules, never sticks). A person looking at the character must feel "that is the logo, walking". Do not copy the logo literally as a flat symbol: re-proportion it into a sweet, balanced, slightly chunky character (about 3 heads tall), with the same softness and the same rounded corner logic.
Dark mode colours (same roles, from my tokens): teal -> #9CE5D6, green -> #9BE3B1, sun -> #FFD28A, ink #F5F8FA, background #111820, surface #1C2732. Light mode: ink #17232D, background #F5F8FA, surface #FFFFFF, plus walk #145AA0 and run #A13F20 as the two accent colours (blue for walking, rust for running: small touches only - the shoes, a headband, the ribbon tip).

## 2. Personality and rules
Name it **Trail** (working name). Warm, encouraging, calm, a little playful; gender-neutral, age-neutral, inclusive; faceless by default (the sun disc has a soft highlight and a gentle inner ring, no features) - the mood comes from posture and motion. Allow a tiny set of optional expression overlays for the head (two small dot eyes, a small smile curve, tired sweat drop, closed eyes for sleep) as separate groups named m-eyes, m-smile, m-sweat so the app can show or hide them. Never aggressive or "extreme sport". Chunky, readable silhouette: it must be recognisable as Trail by silhouette alone, even in one colour (a one-colour silhouette version of each key pose is part of the delivery: the notification icon and widgets use it).

## 5. THE ANIMATION SPEC
1. \`motion.json\`: for every animation: id, which file, trigger (loop | once | state | live), parts, keyframes (offset plus transform / opacity only), transform-origin per part (percent of the part's own box), durationMs, easing, iterations, the reduced-motion still frame.
2. \`character-motion.css\`: the same animations as CSS for the rig, selectors scoped under \`.anim-on\` and per pose (\`.anim-on .pose-walk .m-leg-a\`), with an @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) block; plus a rule so that \`html[data-calm="1"]\` stops everything (the app has a "calm animations" setting).
3. \`preview.html\`: every pose, every animation, light and dark, at 24, 64, 96 and 200 px, with a toggle to switch animation on and off.
4. \`validation.json\`: per file: element and attribute whitelist check (rig), byte size, viewBox, only transform / opacity animated, contrast of the figure against its background >= 3:1, every animation complete as a still frame, and a check that the head, torso and legs are built from the logo shapes.
