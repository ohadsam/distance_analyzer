## 0.6.0
- **Android background service** (the owner's "next step"): 
  - **Workouts keep recording with the screen off.** `TrackingService` (foreground service of type location, visible notification, started only while the app is in front, stops itself after 12 h) writes GPS fixes to a private file (`Tracking`/`FixBuffer`); the page drains them every second (`native.trackDrain`) and feeds them through the same `addFix` as its own GPS (duplicates are rejected by time). Pause and finish stop the service. If the app is killed mid-workout, the fixes recorded meanwhile are applied when the workout is reopened.
  - **All-day steps from the phone's step counter** (`StepStore`, `StepMath` pure + JVM-tested: first reading is a baseline, reboot detected, day roll-over; sampled when the app opens, every minute while in front, and every ~30 minutes by an inexact alarm). The dashboard blends them with workouts: the sensor gives the total, workout steps count in full (weighted), incidental steps at 70 % (`blendDay`).
  - **Notifications while the app is closed** (`NotifyStore`, `NotifyMath` pure + JVM-tested, `NotifyReceiver`, `BootReceiver`): the web app expands fixed reminders for the next 7 days and (when enabled) the recommended walk times from the saved forecast into a flat list (`schedule.js`, `syncNative.js`, texts in the current language) and hands it over; Android stores it, wakes with an inexact alarm (`setAndAllowWhileIdle`, no exact-alarm permission), shows what is due, re-arms the next one and after a reboot; tapping opens the right screen (`#/reco`, `#/workout/walk`).
  - Settings → "Android background" (only in the app) asks for notification and step-counter permissions.
  - Bridge additions are validated (sizes, id/href patterns, bounded counts); new permissions: `FOREGROUND_SERVICE`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION`, `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`, `ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION`, `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`.
- Review fixes (10 findings): the permission question is asked at most 5 times, 8 s apart; stopping goes through the service itself (it answers its start request first, so a quick pause cannot crash it) and the buffer is no longer cleared on stop; a reopened workout stops the service; one GPS source at a time (the service's fixes while it is on); location callbacks and file writes run on a background thread; the step alarm exists only while the sensor and permission exist; a reboot is recognised by the boot time even when the counter already passed the old value; notifications missed while the phone was off are shown after boot; a tapped notification is delivered at once when the app is already in front; the Settings buttons refresh when the app comes back from the permission dialog.
- **Known limits:** the smart "no movement for a while" nudge still needs live data and works only while the app is open (the fixed reminders and the recommended-time notifications do work when closed, with the forecast as of the last time the app was open); an inexact alarm can be late by minutes in Doze; step totals before the first reading of a day are unknown.
- Verified: unit tests (blend, schedule expansion, bounds), Kotlin JVM tests written (`FixBufferTest`, `StepMathTest`, `NotifyMathTest`; run in CI), e2e with a stand-in Android bridge (service started/stopped on start/pause/resume/finish, fixes drained without page GPS, recovery after a reload, permissions block only in the app, schedule handed over, notification tap routing, step counter in the dashboard). **Not verified here:** everything that needs a real phone - the Kotlin build, foreground-service start rules, battery use, the sensor, Doze behaviour, the notification permission dialogs.

## 0.5.0
- **Quick actions** at the top of the dashboard and on the Routes screen: **Measure now** (starts at once with your last activity, records distance and path), **My routes**, and the **suggested route** for now.
- **Live map** in workout mode (`ui/livemap.js`): your path so far, the start, your position, and the route line when following one. Drawn **without map tiles**: real map backgrounds need a second internet use (tile provider) and are waiting for the owner's decision (ROADMAP D2).
- **Routes** (`#/routes`, widget with drill-down, search, Recommendations screen "Routes" tab): after a workout, StepUp recognises a saved route (same direction, similar length, near the start and end, and the sampled points stay on the line both ways) or offers to **save the path as a route** (name, "one-time route" for a trip or holiday). Routes can be renamed, marked one-time, deleted (workouts stay) and earlier workouts can be linked to a route in one tap. Any past workout can be saved as a route from its page.
- **Progress per route**: attempts, fastest and average pace, a pace chart, the trend (first three vs last three attempts, in %), and where you do best (time of day, weather). After a workout on a route: "your Nth time", "new personal best", faster/slower than your average here.
- **Route suggestions** (`routeReco.js`): score = performance in a similar context (45: your speed index on that route at this time of day and in this weather vs the route's own median) + how often you do it (20) + fit to your usual distance (20) + not done too recently (15), with the reasons in words. **One-time routes never take part.**
- **Follow a route** ("Start this route"): the route is drawn, and a **ghost** of your best attempt tells you whether you are ahead of or behind your best by N metres.
- **Weather per workout, automatically** when the online forecast is on and a forecast for that hour is saved (otherwise add it by hand).
- Review fixes (11 findings): the database upgrade no longer leaves a session on memory-only storage when an old tab blocks it (it retries, and our connection yields to newer versions); a workout is patched by reading/writing just that record (linking 50 workouts no longer rescans everything); following a route reads only the best attempt's points; the "Nth attempt / personal best / faster than average" lines compare only with EARLIER attempts and never overwrite a stored link; a followed route only counts when the path really matched; walks and runs are never mixed in route statistics or the ghost; the ghost ignores pauses/gaps and is scaled to the attempt's own distance and is announced only when it changes side; "measure now" forgets its `/go` address so Back or a reload never starts a second recording; one weather mapping, one `median`; neutral wording for the attempt number.
- IndexedDB upgraded to version 2 (new `routes` store; old data untouched). Workout records may carry `routeId`.
- Verified: unit tests (simplify, matching with noise/other street/reverse/partial, ghost distance, sanitisers, progress, best context, suggestions incl. one-time and recency), e2e (save + link + progress + rename + one-time + delete; suggestions by time of day; quick action starts at once; live map; ghost), axe on the new screens. **Not verified here:** real GPS tracks (matching thresholds - 35 m average / 120 m max, 150 m start - are tuned on synthetic tracks), map tiles (not built), Kotlin build in CI.

## 0.4.0
- **Recommendations** (star button in the top bar, dashboard widget with drill-down, screen `#/reco` with filters All / Weather / Effort / Routine):
  - **Best times to walk or run by weather and routine.** `reco.js` scores every forecast hour for the activity (apparent temperature, rain, wind, UV, darkness; a runner tolerates less heat than a walker), learns the person's routine from the last 8 weeks (usual start hours per weekday, sessions per day, typical duration; recent workouts count more) and proposes: **your usual time** (good weather near your usual hours; the window moves to the best nearby hour when the usual one is rainy or hot), **good times** by weather alone (neighbouring hours merged), and **better to avoid** stretches with the main reason (heat, rain, wind, dark). Today/tomorrow, walk/run, an hourly comfort chart, reasons in words.
  - **Effort, steps and routine recommendations** (`generalRecs`): behind the bar late in the week (how many minutes a day), goal reached, very heavy week, low steps in the evening, time to restart, streak.
  - **Notification before your usual time** when the weather is good (opt-in in Settings, once a day per slot).
- **Network, opt-in and minimal** (decision D1 taken by the owner's request; ARCHITECTURE explains): `INTERNET` permission and CSP `connect-src https://api.open-meteo.com`; ONE request type, `GET` forecast with the approximate location rounded to 2 decimals (about 1 km); nothing before the person agrees (consent card, then Settings -> Recommendations); cached for an hour, 8 s timeout, 200 KB cap, no credentials or referrer; the answer is normalised to clamped numbers; "Forget my location" in Settings turns it all off. Attribution "Weather data: Open-Meteo.com" is shown.
- Review fixes (10 findings): the forecast is requested in GMT with epoch seconds (no timezone/DST mix-ups between the place and the phone); after a failed request nothing is asked again for 15 minutes and the retry button forces one; the notification check runs every 5 minutes, needs weather comfort (not only the routine) and uses local clock hours (DST-safe); "your usual time" is learned per activity (a runner at 6 and a walker at 19); an avoid stretch names its biggest problem over the whole stretch; the dashboard widget reuses the loaded data and a leaving drill-down stops a running load; "Forget my location" button; one history read per draw.
- Alerts inbox updates live when a new alert arrives; search finds the recommendations screen and the new settings.
- Verified: unit tests (comfort, routine, windows, hostile forecast answers, URL privacy), e2e with a mocked weather service (no request before consent, rounded coordinates, cache, failure + retry, routine habit windows, widget, settings opt-in/forget, notification once), axe on the new screens. **Not verified here:** the live Open-Meteo answer (the sandbox blocks the host; the format follows its public documentation - first thing to check on a real device), Kotlin build in CI, notifications while the app is closed.

## 0.2.0
- **Design foundation (from the ChatGPT "Momentum" kit, `design/momentum/`)**: eight look packs (momentum, ocean, forest, sunset, violet, slate, mono, high-contrast), each light + dark, generated into `web/css/packs.css` and 54 UI icons into `web/js/iconData.js` by `npm run design`. A unit test re-checks WCAG contrast of every pack and that the generated files are current. Working name "Momentum" is the kit's, not the app's: the app name is still open.
- **Workout mode** (walk / run): GPS distance with accuracy and jump filtering, moving time, current + average pace, km splits (interpolated), kcal estimate, pause/resume, finish with confirmation, summary with a tile-less route drawing, history and detail, delete. One screen, three numbers, three buttons; **screen lock** that needs a press-and-hold; the screen stays awake; a workout in progress survives a reload (it waits paused, so the gap is not counted).
- **Smart rhythm coach (metronome)**: one tap turns it on. It listens to the footsteps with the motion sensor, stays silent for 20 s while it learns, and once the cadence has been steady for 8 s it starts clicking at that cadence (look-ahead scheduler on the Web Audio clock) with a calm pulse animation and a breathing guide (default in 3 steps, out 2; accent on the first beat of each cycle). It shows whether you are on rhythm / a little fast / a little slow, re-locks only after a clearly new steady cadence (16 s), goes silent again when you stop or pause, and has a by-hand rhythm (± buttons, default in Settings) when the phone has no motion sensor. Settings: breathing pattern, click volume, default rhythm.
- Android: `ACCESS_FINE/COARSE_LOCATION` (asked at first workout, granted to our own origin only via `onGeolocationPermissionsShowPrompt`), `NativeBridge.keepAwake(Boolean)`. Still no `INTERNET`.
- Tests: unit (geo/workout, cadence on synthetic accelerometer signals, breathing pattern, design tokens) and e2e with mocked GPS and a fake footstep sensor on a fake clock (learning silence, lock, pause, no-sensor fallback, lock screen, resume after reload, settings), axe at zero for every screen incl. workout + rhythm and three packs, 320 px.
- Verified: static checks, unit tests, e2e in Chromium. **Not verified here (needs a real phone / CI):** the Kotlin build, real GPS accuracy, real accelerometer cadence detection while running (thresholds are tuned on synthetic data), audio timing on a device, battery use. Known limit: tracking and the metronome run only while the screen is on and the app is in front (a foreground location service is the next step).
