# Changelog

## 0.3.0
- **Brand and design (ChatGPT "stepup-final" kit, `design/stepup/`)**: the StepUp symbol (a rising two-step route with an arrow) and Latin wordmark drawn inline in the top bar (`brand.js`, takes the pack colours, never mirrored), Android adaptive launcher icon (foreground/background/monochrome) and legacy icons, PWA icons and maskable icon, all generated by `npm run design` / `npm run icons`. New art for walk/run/weather/badges/states/backgrounds is stored in `design/stepup/svg` for later screens.
- **Dashboard** (home): eight widgets - weekly effort, steps, distance, pace, consistency, weather and performance, personal bests, alerts. Filters (period, activity, weather, text), customise mode (hide/show, move up/down; saved), and **every widget opens a drill-down** with a bigger chart and the workouts behind it.
- **Effort bar** (`effort.js`): weekly target by age (WHO-based guidance) and history (median of recent weeks +10 %), effort minutes by activity, status ahead/on/behind. **Deviation alerts** after a workout: much longer than usual, much faster, or a surge week - each explains what happened, why it matters, what to do, and offers to raise the bar gently (a fifth of the overshoot, at most 10 %) with one tap. Setting to turn it off.
- **Steps** (`steps.js`): estimated from tracked workouts, weighted by speed and continuity (an unbroken brisk bout counts more); recommended steps for today from your own history; shown in the steps widget and feeding the story of the effort bar. (A passive all-day step counter needs the Android sensor: not yet.)
- **Reminders**: fixed reminders for walks and movement breaks (time + days), and optional **smart nudges** when there was no movement for a long time (waking hours, daily cap, spacing, off during a workout and after the goal). Delivered to the alerts inbox and as system notifications when allowed, while the app is open.
- **Alerts inbox** (bell with unread dot) and **global search** (settings in both languages, screens, widgets, workouts, alerts; Hebrew vowel marks and final letters handled; results open the right place and focus the setting).
- **Weather**: add the weather to a workout by hand; weather widget (pace by temperature band/rain), weather filter. Automatic weather needs the internet and is waiting for the owner's decision (ROADMAP D1).
- **Docs for continuity**: `docs/CONTEXT.md` (start here, file map, data model, concepts), `docs/TESTING.md`, `CLAUDE.md`; `docs.test.js` fails when a source file is missing from the map. Settings gained profile (age, height, weight).
- Fixed on the way: the theme pack could be set to "undefined" (default tokens not applied) - now covered by an e2e check of the real `--primary` colour.
- Verified: static checks, 42 unit tests, e2e in Chromium incl. axe over every new screen. Not verified here: Kotlin build, real-device behaviour, notifications while the app is closed.

## 0.2.0
- **Design foundation (from the ChatGPT "Momentum" kit, `design/momentum/`)**: eight look packs (momentum, ocean, forest, sunset, violet, slate, mono, high-contrast), each light + dark, generated into `web/css/packs.css` and 54 UI icons into `web/js/iconData.js` by `npm run design`. A unit test re-checks WCAG contrast of every pack and that the generated files are current. Working name "Momentum" is the kit's, not the app's: the app name is still open.
- **Workout mode** (walk / run): GPS distance with accuracy and jump filtering, moving time, current + average pace, km splits (interpolated), kcal estimate, pause/resume, finish with confirmation, summary with a tile-less route drawing, history and detail, delete. One screen, three numbers, three buttons; **screen lock** that needs a press-and-hold; the screen stays awake; a workout in progress survives a reload (it waits paused, so the gap is not counted).
- **Smart rhythm coach (metronome)**: one tap turns it on. It listens to the footsteps with the motion sensor, stays silent for 20 s while it learns, and once the cadence has been steady for 8 s it starts clicking at that cadence (look-ahead scheduler on the Web Audio clock) with a calm pulse animation and a breathing guide (default in 3 steps, out 2; accent on the first beat of each cycle). It shows whether you are on rhythm / a little fast / a little slow, re-locks only after a clearly new steady cadence (16 s), goes silent again when you stop or pause, and has a by-hand rhythm (± buttons, default in Settings) when the phone has no motion sensor. Settings: breathing pattern, click volume, default rhythm.
- Android: `ACCESS_FINE/COARSE_LOCATION` (asked at first workout, granted to our own origin only via `onGeolocationPermissionsShowPrompt`), `NativeBridge.keepAwake(Boolean)`. Still no `INTERNET`.
- Tests: unit (geo/workout, cadence on synthetic accelerometer signals, breathing pattern, design tokens) and e2e with mocked GPS and a fake footstep sensor on a fake clock (learning silence, lock, pause, no-sensor fallback, lock screen, resume after reload, settings), axe at zero for every screen incl. workout + rhythm and three packs, 320 px.
- Verified: static checks, unit tests, e2e in Chromium. **Not verified here (needs a real phone / CI):** the Kotlin build, real GPS accuracy, real accelerometer cadence detection while running (thresholds are tuned on synthetic data), audio timing on a device, battery use. Known limit: tracking and the metronome run only while the screen is on and the app is in front (a foreground location service is the next step).
