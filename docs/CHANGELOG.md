# Changelog

## 0.24.0
- **Permissions in one place**: Settings opens with a Permissions section listing location, notifications, steps and (Android) running in the background, each with its state (Allowed / Not allowed yet / Blocked) and an **Allow** button. When Android will not ask again the button becomes **Open settings**; the list redraws by itself when you come back. In a browser the same section covers location and notifications. A toggle chooses whether the app asks at start-up.
- **A sheet at start-up** when something is missing: one dialog (once per session, never over a workout, with "Allow all", "Not now" and "Do not ask again") that asks for the permissions right there.
- **On by default** (owner's decision): the street map, the weather forecast and the route planner's online services; the location is found by itself once the permission is granted (rounded position, refreshed every 6 hours). All can still be turned off in Settings; the sheet and the guide say that they use online services. *Existing installs that never touched these switches now have them on.*
- **Update that installs over the app** (Android): the update box at the top of Settings checks for a new version and, with one tap, downloads it, checks its checksum, package, version and signing key, and installs it as an **update** (your data stays; no uninstall). Android asks once to allow installing updates from StepUp; the page carries on by itself when you return. Progress and every kind of failure are explained, with a manual download link. In a browser the button still refreshes the web app.
- Android: `REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES` (the update), new bridge methods `permissionState`, `requestPermission`, `openAppSettings`, `updateStart`, `updateRunning`; Kotlin `PermMath`, `UpdateMath`, `Updater` + `InstallReceiver`, with JVM tests.
- Search finds "permissions" and "update"; the guide has a "Permissions and updates" step (screenshots regenerated).
- Verified: unit tests (permission list and rules, defaults, the position refresh), e2e (permissions screen and sheet on the Android stand-in and in a browser, defaults, the whole update flow on the stand-in), axe. **Not verified without a phone**: the real Android dialogs, the installer, a real download (Kotlin is compiled and unit-tested by CI only).

## 0.14.0
- **Street map (opt-in, decision D2)**: Settings -> "Show a street map behind routes and the live map" (off by default). On: the finished-workout picture, the saved-route picture and the workout summary show OpenStreetMap tiles under your path, and the **live map follows you** at street level. Already cheap and unchanged: one inexact notification alarm at a time, no polling, no wake locks outside a workout, the forecast cached for an hour with a 15-minute failure back-off.

New: `records.js`, `podiumCard.js`, `ui/podium.js`; Android: `FileProvider` (two cache folders only), no CAMERA permission (the camera app takes the picture), `PhotoMath`. The polished podium/medal/trophy art is requested in `docs/prompts/ROUND6_PROMPT.md`; this version draws a simple one in code.
